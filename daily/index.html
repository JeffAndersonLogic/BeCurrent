Follow one story you actually care about.

Politics is fine. So are science, technology, culture, courts, money, local government, conflict or another story worth knowing. Record the headline, outlet, date and link in your Canvas News Log.

What actually happened in Your Pick?

Give the event in two sentences in your own words. Do not retell the article; identify the change, decision, discovery or event.

WRITE IN CANVAS · Your Pick → Story details + What happened?

Which of today’s two stories deserves more attention — The Lead or Your Pick — and why?

Choose one and defend the judgment with specific evidence or consequences. Your reasoning matters more than matching anyone else’s choice.
