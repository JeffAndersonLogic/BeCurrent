What students will learn

How members of Congress are chosen, what a majority can do, how institutions check each other, and how to distinguish election reporting from prediction.

What this unit is not

It does not recommend parties or candidates or grade students on political opinions. Historical and institutional facts come from primary government references and cited reporting.
