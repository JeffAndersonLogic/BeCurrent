Unit 2 · Who Has the Power? · 2026 Midterms

If the president isn't on the ballot,
why does this election matter?

Every two years, Americans choose a new House of Representatives and part of the Senate. The president is not up for election in 2026. So what can change in Washington on November 3?

435 voting House seats6 years a Senate termNov. 3 2026 federal election

Your investigation

Five chapters. One big question.

This investigation is about institutions and decisions, not telling you which party or candidate to support. Look at what offices are elected, what Congress can do, and which claims news reports actually establish.

Today's learning goal: Explain how a congressional election can affect national decisions without electing a president.
