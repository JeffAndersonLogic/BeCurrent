Reverse History

Today's event is the starting line, not the whole story.

BeCurrent begins with a real current event and keeps moving backward until the history explains how the present became possible. It is not a hunt for vaguely similar events from the past.

  1. 1What is happening now?Start with the current event.
  2. 2Why now?Find the immediate decisions and pressures.
  3. 3What changed earlier?Trace the turning points that shaped those choices.
  4. 4Where do the roots begin?Go back only as far as the story requires.
