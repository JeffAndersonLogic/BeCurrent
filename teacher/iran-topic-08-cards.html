Topic 8 · Card Sort

One set per student or pair. Cut on the dashed lines. Students sort the eight turning points under the two pile cards, pick the three that best explain this week’s headline, and put those three in order. The pile a card lands in matters less than the reason for it.

Long-running conflict

A fear or grudge that kept going for decades.

Recent decision

A choice leaders made in the last few years that could have gone another way.

1953

Coup removes Mosaddegh

The U.S. and Britain help overthrow Iran’s prime minister. Iranians remember foreign powers taking away their government.

1979

Revolution and hostage crisis

Allies become enemies. Each side builds a memory of the other as the aggressor.

1980–88

Iran-Iraq War

Iran survives an eight-year invasion and builds missiles, the IRGC and armed partners abroad so it is never that exposed again.

2015

The nuclear deal

Nuclear limits and inspections are traded for sanctions relief. A deal turns out to be possible.

2018

U.S. leaves the deal

Sanctions return. Iran later exceeds the nuclear limits, and trust drops further.

2024

First direct attacks

Iran and Israel strike each other’s territory. The shadow war becomes open, state-to-state fighting.

2025

A bigger war

Israel and Iran fight a sustained war, and the U.S. bombs Iranian nuclear sites.

FEB 2026

The current war begins

A joint U.S.-Israeli offensive starts the present war. Iran retaliates and shipping through Hormuz collapses.
