TEACHER RUN OF SHOW / AI1 / 2026-11-09

What AI Actually Is

How can a computer sound like it understands you?

Class opening

I used AI to help build parts of this unit. As you watch, what would convince you that an AI-generated answer is accurate?

Timing / 55-minute before-lunch route

0-1 min teacher hook and start film immediately; 1-54:30 min complete NOVA documentary (53:30); 54:30-55 min closing cue. No eight-question worksheet, MagicSchool lab, or AI1 Canvas submission on film day.

Learning objective

  • I can explain patterns, training examples, and prediction in plain language.
  • I can tell when a tool likely uses AI and when the answer depends on its implementation.
  • I can explain why a convincing sentence might still be incorrect.

Viewing priorities

  1. The question hiding in your phone. After the teacher's opening, write one private prediction: what do you think AI actually learns?
  2. Watch the documentary: Where did AI come from?. Watch for: What did machines learn from, and what were humans still responsible for?
  3. Become the predictor. Compare the predictable starter with the one that depends most on the person.
  4. AI or just a computer program?. Sort eight technologies into likely AI, likely rule-based, or depends.
  5. Ask the chatbot, then check. Return to your first prediction. What has changed?

Clip and fallback

A.I. Revolution | opening chapters ↗; assigned: Full film / 00:00-53:30. Where does AI learn patterns? What can it help people do? What can go wrong? Who should remain responsible?

Preview playback, duration and caption availability before students arrive; printed chapter notes are the fallback. Avoid video embedding.

Privacy and access

Never type your full name, address, or anything private. Your teacher can read this chat. MagicSchool join information goes in Canvas only; not in GitHub code or the public website.

Important: This teacher run-of-show page contains NO answer keys or confidential student information. GitHub Pages URLs are public even when no student link is displayed.

Submission
