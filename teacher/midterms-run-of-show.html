How the two screens stay aligned

Keep the Control Room on your computer and open Projector in a second browser tab or window on the same computer. Advancing either window updates the other through browser storage. Different devices are not synchronized. Teacher guidance is shown only in the Control Room.

What happens to student work?

The existing student page autosaves locally on each Chromebook. Students gather their answers and submit in Canvas Text Entry. This teacher page does not collect student answers or make Canvas assignments.
