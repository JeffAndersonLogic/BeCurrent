TEACHER RUN OF SHOW / AI2 / 2026-11-19

What Exactly AI Is

How can a computer sound like it understands you?

Class opening

What did NOVA show about how AI learns? Use the five chapters and practical examples to explain what makes AI different from an ordinary fixed-rule program.

Timing / 55-minute before-lunch route

0–5 min open AI1 independently; 5–17 min chapters 1–2; 17–32 min chapter 3 and sort; 32–47 min chapters 4–5 with printed samples or optional chatbot; 47–55 min Gather All My Work and Canvas filing. Follow the independent readings and examples.

Learning objective

  • I can explain patterns, training examples, and prediction in plain language.
  • I can tell when a tool likely uses AI and when the answer depends on its implementation.
  • I can explain why a convincing sentence might still be incorrect.

Five beats of the story

  1. From the NOVA documentary to your own examples. Read the background, think about your own examples, and answer Question 1. Continue through the chapters in order.
  2. Be the Model: Predict the next word. Complete the five starters privately, then answer Questions 2 and 3.
  3. Patterns learned from examples. Try the eight-card AI/not-AI sort, read the feedback, and answer Questions 4 and 5.
  4. Chatbot or printed sample: Compare the replies. Compare either the chatbot or the printed sample to your predictions. Answer Questions 6 and 7. No live chatbot access is required.
  5. Checkpoint: When should we trust the machine?. Answer Question 8, review all answers, then Gather All My Work and submit to Canvas.

Clip and fallback

A.I. Revolution — Full NOVA Documentary (previous class) ↗; assigned: Optional review of the complete film shown on AI1 November 17. No replay required during AI2.. Where does AI learn patterns? What can it help people do? What can go wrong? Who should remain responsible?

Preview playback, duration and caption availability before students arrive; printed chapter notes are the fallback. Avoid video embedding.
