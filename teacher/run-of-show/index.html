Iran at War

8-part sequence · current build

Day
Current Anchor

The War Now

Orient students to the decision-makers, Iran-backed armed partners, regional geography, and the Strait of Hormuz. Begin the PBS FRONTLINE documentary with minimal note-taking.

Teaching focus: comprehension before historical explanation

Historical Reverse

1953 + 1979

Run of Show standard for every lesson

Each dedicated teacher page should eventually include: before-class setup, target timing by segment, exact transition language where useful, video start/stop cues, discussion prompts, likely misconceptions, IEP/504 load-reduction options, what to cut first if time runs short, and the one thing students must leave understanding.

Future units plug in here.
