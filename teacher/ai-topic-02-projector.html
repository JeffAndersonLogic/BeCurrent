AI2 / WRAP-UP

Wrap-up

Pick one question. Think it through, or jot your answer in your notes.

Quick check

  1. How often did you guess the machine's next word? What does that say about its answers?
  2. Finish it: "The chatbot is good at ___ but not at ___."
  3. Which red line is easiest to break by accident?

Go deeper

  1. Which answer sounded sure but shouldn't have? What would a careful answer sound like?
  2. Why doesn't asking the chatbot again count as checking it?
  3. Was the bot easier to catch on a topic you know well? What does that mean for topics you don't?
  4. When is a good guess good enough, and when isn't it?
