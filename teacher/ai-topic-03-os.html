TEACHER RUN OF SHOW / AI3 / 2026-11-13

What AI Changes, and Who Pays

Who benefits from AI, and who absorbs its costs?

Class opening

An AI answer starts on your phone. How does it connect to a construction site in Lebanon, Indiana?

Timing / 55-minute before-lunch route

0-4 min opening; 4-15 min read and orient; 15-29 min evidence investigation; 29-40 min practice and answers; 40-50 min checkpoint/review; 50-55 min Canvas filing.

Learning objective

  • I can trace AI activity through computers and local infrastructure.
  • I can distinguish company promises from measured outcomes.
  • I can explain why different sources emphasize different facts.

Five beats of the story

  1. A job is made of tasks. Do not treat what a system can produce as the same thing as what it should be trusted to decide.
  2. Two headlines, two measures. Ask what each headline counts, compared with what, and over which period.
  3. The cloud needs a building. Trace: model demand -> data center -> electricity / cooling -> local decisions.
  4. A field in Lebanon, Indiana. Distinguish temporary construction work from permanent jobs and claimed protections.
  5. Tradeoffs and whose frame wins. Construct a claim with one benefit, one potential burden, and a source for each.

Preview playback, duration and caption availability before students arrive; printed chapter notes are the fallback. Avoid video embedding.
