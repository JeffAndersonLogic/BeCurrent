TEACHER RUN OF SHOW / AI3 / 2026-11-23

When AI Gets it Wrong

Who should check a convincing answer?

Class opening

In 2023, lawyers handed a judge court cases that a chatbot had made up, and nobody checked. Today you go hunting for the same kind of mistake: get a MagicSchool chatbot talking about something you know well, then check every claim it makes outside the chat.

Your opener: the overview story

  • Last class you found out a chatbot builds its answers by predicting likely words. Today: what happens when those likely words are wrong.
  • In 2023, lawyers in a federal case handed a judge legal research full of court cases that did not exist. A chatbot had made them up, and nobody checked. The judge sanctioned them.
  • An invented detail stated as fact is called a hallucination. Today you go hunting for them, about something you know a lot about.
  • You ask the bot for a quote, a number, some sources and recent news, and you check every answer outside the chat: confirmed, wrong, or can't confirm. Asking the bot again does not count.
  • Getting in, on the projector: open today's AI3 assignment in Canvas, follow the MagicSchool link, open Hallucination Hunt, and type READY. Keep a second tab open for checking. HELP works at any time.
  • When the bot sends you back, write on the BeCurrent page. The chat is not your submission; the Canvas paste is.

Timing / 55-minute before-lunch route

0–5 min: your opener (below), ending with a one-minute demo of getting into the room on the projector. 5–9 min: Pick Your Turf and the Hunter's Rules in the chat. 9–25 min: Rounds 1–2, then Questions 1–3. Walk the room here: this is where students need help finding a reliable source, and where they try to "check" by asking the bot again. 25–37 min: Round 3 and the lawyer reading, then Questions 4–5. 37–47 min: Round 4 and the hiring-tool and robocall readings, then Questions 6–7. 47–55 min: Round 5, Question 8 and Gather All My Work. Students who do not finish submit to Canvas before next class (the board's homework line already says so). Fast finishers type BONUS. If there is no time for the closing think-aloud, open AI4 with it.

MagicSchool chatbot setup

Create one custom chatbot in the BeCurrent MagicSchool room and paste these four fields. The instructions tell the bot to answer normally and never make mistakes on purpose, so every catch a student makes is a real result. Students need a second tab with web access to check answers. Put the room link in the Canvas assignment, never on this public site.

If MagicSchool is down

If MagicSchool is down for everyone: run the hunt yourself on the projector in MagicSchool or another district-approved chatbot. Pick a topic yourself, type a request for a quote, a number and two sources one at a time, and have every student check each answer on their own device and write down a verdict. Students answer Questions 1–5 from what they see. For Round 4, type one recent-news question and have students check it with a real outlet. Questions 6–8 come from the page readings. Skip the bonus quests.

Closing think-aloud

When someone uses an AI answer that turns out to be wrong, who is responsible: the company that built it, the AI, or the person who used it? Read three or four answers aloud with names removed, then answer it yourself out loud. Nobody is called on. It previews the question AI5 asks about who makes the rules.

Learning objective

  • I can find a reliable source outside a chatbot and use it to check an AI claim.
  • I can decide whether an AI claim is confirmed, wrong, or cannot be confirmed.
  • I can tell a hallucination apart from bias and from a deliberate fake.

Five beats of the story

  1. Before you open MagicSchool: Sounding sure is not being right. Listen to the opener. Then open the BeCurrent MagicSchool room from today's Canvas assignment, choose Hallucination Hunt, and type READY. Keep this page and a second tab for checking open beside the chat.
  2. Rounds 1 and 2: The quote hunt and the number hunt. Play Rounds 1 and 2. Check each answer outside the chat and give it a verdict. When the chatbot tells you to pause, answer Questions 1–3 here, then type NEXT in the chat.
  3. Round 3: The source hunt, and the lawyers who did not check. Play Round 3 and check every source outside the chat. When the chatbot tells you to pause, answer Questions 4 and 5 here, then type NEXT.
  4. Round 4: The news hunt, and two failures that are not hallucinations. Play Round 4 and check one news claim with a real outlet. Read about the hiring tool and the fake robocall. When the chatbot tells you to pause, answer Questions 6 and 7, then type NEXT.
  5. Round 5 and checkpoint: The scoreboard and your rule. Finish Round 5, answer Question 8, review all eight answers, then Gather All My Work and submit to Canvas. Then try a bonus quest.
