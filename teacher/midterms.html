Five complete lessons / Civics

Who has the power?

Teach the institutions, investigate the messages, then return for the 2026 results. Each lesson includes the teaching OS, student activity, and separate projector.

The standing class rhythm

Before lunch: 55 minutes of unit content. Finish the checkpoint and Canvas filing.

Lunch: pause. After lunch: 20–25 minutes of CNN10 and daily reporting. The OS plans 10 minutes for CNN10 and 15 for The Desk; shorten the Desk to 10 when the return block is shorter.

Assessment and preparation

Topics 1–4: five practice choices, a brief source note, an application note, and a final explanation. Topic 5: a guided results notebook and one final explanation. Optional planning notes are not required. Political preference is not scored.

Topic 5 is complete as a results investigation; the class supplies sourced outcomes after Election Day. No results are inferred or fetched automatically.
