Social Media

We are going to figure out how social media actually works. The machine. The business. Not whether it is good or bad for you, and nobody is going to ask you to delete anything. By the end you should be able to explain how the thing works, out loud, to an adult, which is more than most adults can do.

The Blocks The Question
Hold onto this for two weeks

The question this unit ends on

Who should get to decide what a platform is allowed to do to your attention: you, the company, or the government?

You do not have to answer it yet, and you will not be graded on which side you land. You will be graded on whether you can state the strongest version of the argument against you.

The Arc

Five blocks, in order.

Each one uses the earlier ones. The chain you draw in Block 1 is what you check against a real document in Block 4, so they are a sequence rather than a list.

  1. Block 1

    Where the Money Comes From

    Follow the money backwards, one step at a time

    You list the apps you opened today that did not charge you, then build a chain of boxes and arrows from opening an app to a company getting paid. Paper, not devices. Nobody presents; you walk the room and read four other chains instead.

    Competency 1 Competency 5

    Done on paper in class. Nothing to open here.

  2. Block 2

    Somebody Made a Film About This

    Your job is not to agree with it

    We watch it. Your job is to do to the film exactly what you did to your app in Block 1: take it apart, find out how it works, see where the money is.

    Competency 2

    Done on paper in class. Nothing to open here.

  3. Block 3

    Inside the Algorithm Box

    What the app counts, and what it does with the count

    We open the box almost every chain had in the middle. Read the Brief, then predict what the app counts about you before we check.

    Competency 1 Competency 4
    Read the Brief
  4. Block 4

    Go Check It Yourself

    Reading a privacy policy as a primary source

    You check the chain you drew in Block 1 against the company's own privacy policy. Bring a device for this one, because the document is the assignment.

    Competency 2 Competency 5
    Read the Brief
  5. Block 5

    Who Gets to Decide?

    You, the company, or the government

    The question from day one, argued properly. You state the strongest version of a position you do not hold, then take your own.

    Competency 3 Competency 4 Competency 5
    Read the Brief
