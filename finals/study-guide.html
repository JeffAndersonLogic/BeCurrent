Practice the skills across units

  • What happened, and what is someone claiming about it?
  • What evidence supports that claim? What can the source establish?
  • Which earlier decision or condition helps explain the present?
  • What is the strongest competing explanation?
  • What remains uncertain, and what would you check next?

Use specific examples from lessons. Political opinions are not the basis of the grade; explain your reasoning with evidence.
