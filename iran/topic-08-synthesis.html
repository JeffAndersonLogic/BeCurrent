Iran at War · Topic 8 · Return to today · Causation synthesis

When Did This War Really Begin?

“The Iran War began on February 28, 2026” is factually correct. It is also historically incomplete. Your final job is to decide which earlier turning point best explains why that date became possible.

Essential question

Was the 2026 Iran War mainly the result of recent decisions, or decades of unresolved conflict?

You are graded on the strength of the causal argument, not on choosing a particular turning point.

The rule
A date becomes a cause only when you can explain the mechanism.
Step 2 · Build the chain forward

Put the story in chronological order.

You started with the 2026 war, uncovered an older root, and then followed the chain forward. Now make the causal links visible: where did one development change the conditions for the next?

1953

U.S.- and British-backed covert intervention helps remove Mosaddegh

Foreign intervention becomes part of Iranian political memory; the Shah's position is strengthened.

1979

Islamic Revolution and Hostage Crisis

The U.S.-Iran alliance collapses. Revolutionary distrust of the United States and American distrust of the new Iranian system reinforce one another.

1980–88

Iran-Iraq War

Iran survives a long invasion. The IRGC grows, and deterrence, missiles, Gulf strategy and influence beyond Iran become increasingly important.

2015

JCPOA

A limited bargain exchanges nuclear restrictions and inspections for sanctions relief without resolving the wider rivalry.

2018

United States leaves the JCPOA

Sanctions return; Iran later exceeds several nuclear limits; the nuclear problem again becomes more acute.

2024

Iran and Israel exchange direct attacks

A longstanding shadow conflict crosses the state-to-state threshold more than once.

2025

Major Israel-Iran war and U.S. strikes on nuclear sites

Direct military action becomes larger and more sustained.

FEB 28, 2026

The current war begins

A new joint U.S.-Israeli offensive opens the present conflict. Iran retaliates, and Hormuz becomes central to the wider economic fight.

Checkpoint 1 · Causal chain

Choose three consecutive stops and write the arrows between them.

Use the structure: Event A changes ___, which makes Event B more likely because ___; Event B then changes ___.

Step 3 · Rank the causes

Not every turning point deserves equal weight.

A strong historical explanation makes choices. Rank the three moments that do the most work in explaining 2026.

Checkpoint 2 · Rank the causes

1st, 2nd, 3rd—and why.

Your candidates may include 1953, 1979, 1980–88, 2015, 2018, 2024, 2025 or February 2026. Give one sentence of causal reasoning for each.

Teacher view: A student can reasonably rank 2018 or 2024 above 1953. Do not reward “older = deeper = more important.” Proximity, mechanism and explanatory reach all matter.
Step 4 · Test your argument

The strongest argument against you should sound strong.

If your answer survives only because you make the other side weak, you have not tested it.

DEEP

Long roots argument

Without the collapse of trust after 1953 and 1979, later nuclear and military crises might have been easier to manage.

MID

Diplomacy argument

The 2015 bargain showed conflict was not inevitable; the 2018 breakdown may therefore carry more causal weight than older grievances.

NEAR

Escalation argument

Whatever the older history, decisions in 2024–2026 crossed thresholds that earlier leaders had managed to avoid.

Complicate your own case before you write.

Choose a turning point you did not rank first. State the best argument for it. Then complete this sentence: “I am not claiming the war literally started in ____. I am claiming ____ is the most important explanatory beginning because...”

Immediate beginning

February 28, 2026

This is when the current war begins as a military event. It is the correct answer to “when did this round of war start?”

VERSUS
Explanatory beginning

An earlier turning point

This answers a different question: when did the relationship, strategy or dispute change in a way that made the later war more likely?

Step 5 · Return to today

When did the 2026 Iran War really begin?

Write one defensible answer using at least three specific pieces of evidence, a clear causal mechanism and one competing interpretation.

Your response needs four moves.

  1. Claim: name the turning point that deserves the most causal weight.
  2. Evidence: use at least three specific developments from the unit.
  3. Reasoning: explain the arrows connecting your evidence to the 2026 war.
  4. Complication: address the strongest competing turning point and explain why yours still does more work.
