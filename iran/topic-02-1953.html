Source check · 5 minutes

A documentary is evidence and interpretation at the same time.

FRONTLINE is a highly reported secondary source. That makes it useful—not infallible.

What it can do well

Build a narrative from many sources.

Interviews, archival footage, reporting, official statements, and chronology can show how events connect.

What we still test

Causal weight.

The film can present strong evidence, but students still decide how much weight different turning points deserve.
