IF YOU MISSED THE FILM / SHORT CONTEXT

AI systems can recognize patterns, generate language, and support decisions. Many systems learn relationships from large training datasets, but different AI tools work differently. A realistic answer can still be inaccurate. People must decide when to trust, check, or limit these systems.

The documentary is an introduction, not an up-to-date list of AI capabilities in November 2026. Later lessons investigate evidence and responsibility in detail.
