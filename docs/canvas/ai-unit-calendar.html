Midterms · After the Vote

Why They Lost, and What Comes Next

OVERVIEW

Two articles from The Week, published after the midterms. The first collects writers' explanations for why Republicans lost the House and Senate. The second covers how President Trump and both parties are responding. You will weigh the explanations, sort reporting from opinion, and take a position using evidence.

STEP 1: Read
  1. Midterms: Explaining the GOP’s crushing defeat (The Week)
  2. Trump and Democrats brace for battle (The Week)

Both articles are mostly a roundup of what different writers think. As you read, notice who is saying each thing and where they write.

STEP 2: Answer

1. Why did Republicans lose? (Causation)
The first article gives several explanations: the Iran war and gas prices, corruption, how Democrats campaigned, Latino voters shifting, and “all the things” in Trump’s second term. Which explanation is backed by the strongest evidence? What evidence would you still want before you were sure?
Start here: “The strongest explanation is ___ because the article shows ___. I would still want to know ___.”

2. Reporting or opinion? (Sourcing)
Both articles quote many writers. Some report what happened, like Trump naming Stephen Miller his chief of staff. Others argue or predict, like the claim that “It was the war, stupid.” Find one more example of each, from either article. How can you tell the difference?
Start here: “One fact is ___. One opinion is ___. I can tell because ___.”

3. Checking power or harassing? (Civics)
Democrats take control of the House and Senate in January and plan investigations, including letters asking companies like Amazon, Apple and Walmart for records. The White House’s lawyer warned that investigations meant to harass could cross a legal line. Congress is supposed to keep a check on the president. Where is the line between doing that job and harassing?
Start here: “An investigation is fair when ___. It turns into harassment when ___.”

4. Did they get the message? (Taking a Position)
The first article says voters were reacting to the war, prices and corruption. The second says Trump responded by promoting Stephen Miller, and House Republicans chose Jim Jordan, a strong Trump supporter, as their leader. Is that response a smart choice or a risky one? Use evidence from both articles.
Start here: “I think their response is ___ because the first article says ___ and the second shows ___.”

STEP 3: Submit

Click Start Assignment and type your answers in the Text Entry box. Number them 1 to 4. Two to four sentences each is enough.

Use the Start here frames if they help. Voice typing is fine.

SUCCESS CRITERIA
  • I answered all four questions.
  • I used a specific detail from the articles in each answer.
  • I explained my thinking, not just my answer.

Your political views are not graded. You are graded on how you use evidence, not on which side you take.
