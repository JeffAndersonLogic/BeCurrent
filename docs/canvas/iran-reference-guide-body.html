Iran at War: Test Reference Guide

What this is: a one-page guide you fill in with your own notes. You may use it on the Iran at War unit test.

Due: Friday, October 2, before the test opens.

Two rules to know before you start

  1. The test stays locked until you turn this in. Once you submit your guide here, the test opens for you.
  2. You may only use the guide you turn in here. Not a different copy, and not other notes.

Step 1: Download the blank guide

[INSERT BLANK PDF FROM THE DOCUMENTS PANEL]

  1. Click the link above.
  2. Click Download. The file saves to the Downloads folder on your Chromebook.

Step 2: Open it in Google Chrome

  1. Open the Files app and click Downloads.
  2. Right-click the guide (tap with two fingers), then choose Open with and Google Chrome.

Use Chrome. The boxes do not work correctly in Kami or in the Canvas preview.

Step 3: Fill in the boxes

  • Click inside a box and type. Press Tab to jump to the next box.
  • Every box has a size limit. When a box stops taking text, it is full. Pick what matters most.
  • The font and text size are locked. You cannot change them, and you cannot paste more than a box can show.
  • Short notes fit best: keywords, arrows (like 1953 → distrust), and abbreviations.
  • Not sure what to include? Use the Iran at War study guide.

Step 4: Save it with your notes in it

  1. Click the Download button (the arrow pointing down) in the top right corner of Chrome. You can also press Ctrl + S.
  2. If Chrome asks, choose With your changes.
  3. Name the file with your name, like Lastname Firstname Reference Guide, and click Save.

Do not just close the tab. If you close it without saving, your notes are gone.

Step 5: Check your saved copy

Open the file you just saved (Files, then Downloads). Make sure your notes are in the boxes. If the boxes are empty, go back to Step 4 and save again.

Step 6: Upload it here

  1. At the top of this page, click Start Assignment.
  2. Click Upload File and choose the guide you saved in Step 4.
  3. Click Submit Assignment.

Step 7: Check what you turned in

After you submit, click your file’s name on the right side of this page. Make sure your notes show up. This is the guide you will use on the test, so check it now.

Need to fix something?

You can turn in a new version until the test opens. Open your saved file in Chrome, make your changes, save it again (Step 4), and upload it again (Step 6). The last file you turn in is the one you use.

On test day

Open this assignment and click your file’s name to open your guide. Use only this guide.

If something goes wrong

  • I cannot type in the boxes. You are not in Chrome. Go back to Step 2.
  • A box stopped taking text. The box is full. Shorten what you wrote.
  • My notes disappeared. The file was saved without your changes. Fill it in again and follow Step 4 carefully.
  • Canvas will not take my file. It must be the PDF from Step 4. Other file types are not accepted.
  • Still stuck? Ask me before the test, not during it.
