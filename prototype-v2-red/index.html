Why is there war with Iran?

The shooting has slowed, but the conflict has not ended. To understand the sanctions, the Strait of Hormuz, the nuclear dispute and the hostility between governments, we have to move backward.

Where the story stands

The war is now a fight over what peace would require.

  • U.S.-Israeli attacks on Iran began February 28.
  • Major combat operations gave way to an unstable ceasefire in April.
  • An interim June agreement unraveled over sanctions, ports and control of the Strait of Hormuz.
  • Washington announced another major sanctions push this week while Tehran warned against outside support for it.
